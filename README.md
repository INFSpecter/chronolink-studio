## Requirements & Setup (Before Running)
1. **Install ADB on macOS:**
   ```bash
   brew install android-platform-tools
   ```
2. **Enable USB Debugging on Android:**
   - Open **Settings → About phone** and tap **Build number** 7 times to unlock Developer Options.
   - Open **Settings → System → Developer options** and turn **USB debugging ON**, then connect your phone to your Mac and tap **Allow**.
3. **Ensure `chronolink` is on your Android device:**
   - The `chronolink` binary must be located at `/data/local/tmp/chronolink` on your phone.

## Core Features
- **10,800-Case Database:** Precomputed lookup matrix across 24 hours, 15 minute brackets, and 10 major timezones running on `chronolink`.
- **Built-in One-Key Self-Updater (`[U]`):** Allows both `INF_Specter` and any user who downloads the release to update to the latest version directly from GitHub.
- **Conversational AI Advisor (`[A]`), World Clocks (`[W]`), Diagnostics (`[D]`), and Persistent Worked/Failed Stats (`[Y]`/`[N]`/`[L]`).**

## How to Install & Auto-Update
1. Download **`Chronolink Studio.zip`** from the **Releases** sidebar on the right and double-click to unzip it.
2. Double-click **`Chronolink Studio.app`** on macOS.
3. Press **`U`** anytime inside the app to automatically download and apply future updates published by **INF_Specter**.
